    Supbyte Sound Booster Help

    Welcome to Sound Booster! Enhance your audio experience with powerful controls. Let's get you started with the basics.

    Main Controls

    • Volume Slider: Adjust the volume from 0% to 600% to boost or reduce audio levels for clearer, louder sound.
    • Audio Profile: Choose from presets like Flat, Voice, Bass, or Treble. Use the save button (✔️) to remember your settings for this site.
    • Output Device: Select your preferred speaker or audio device. Volume auto-adjusts for safety when switching; save your choice per tab with the ✔️ button.
